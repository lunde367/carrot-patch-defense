Carrot Patch Defense: Help

Stuck on a garden, lost a purchase, or found a bug? Answers to common questions are below. For anything else, leave a note here and say which phone you're using.

I keep losing a level. What can I do?

Spend your stars in the Garden Shed (Upgrades): those upgrades help in every level. Replay gardens you've beaten to earn challenge stars ✦, and spend them on Skills to make a defender stronger for good. You can also switch to Easy any time in Settings.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted too, and it doesn't move to a new phone by itself.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

How do I replay the tutorial?

Settings → Tutorial → Replay.

The screen moves too much for me.

Turn on Settings → Reduce motion to stop screen shake, confetti and page-turn animations.

What does the game collect?

No personal information. See the privacy page for the details.
