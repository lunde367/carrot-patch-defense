Carrot Patch Defense: Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Carrot Patch Defense is a garden game. It has no accounts and asks for no personal information. This page explains, in plain words, everything the game stores and sends.

Your progress stays on your device

Stars, upgrades, journal pages, settings and the gardener's name you choose are saved only on your phone, tablet or browser. They are not sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing the browser's site data) deletes them.

Anonymous play statistics

To help balance the levels, the game can send a few anonymous facts about play, such as "level 7 won with 3 stars on Normal" or "the tutorial was finished". These facts:

  • never include your name, the gardener's name, an account, an advertising ID or any device or install ID;
  • can't be linked to you or to each other;
  • are sent over a secure connection without cookies.

You can turn this off any time in Settings → Share anonymous play data.

Ads

The phone apps offer optional video ads that you choose to watch for a reward, like extra seeds. Nothing plays unless you tap an ad button. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Google's practices are described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can limit ad tracking in your phone's settings. The website version shows no ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. The game never sees your payment details; it only learns whether the purchase was made.

Children

The game is made to be enjoyed by all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the support page.
